# Seeing What an Initiative Delivers

One initiative can drive several deliverables: a programme of work might go live on a mobile app, a gateway and a back-office system. The link is made on each [lifecycle segment](lifecycle-segments.md), which names both the deliverable it belongs to and the initiative behind it. The timeline gives you two ways to see those links.

## The deliverable count on an initiative

An initiative bar linked to at least one lifecycle segment shows a small badge with a link icon and a number: how many **different deliverables** it drives. Three go-lives on the same application count as one.

Click the badge to list those deliverables, each with the asset it sits under. Choose one and the timeline selects the initiative and scrolls to that deliverable's earliest segment, with the highlight below already on.

The badge appears in every grouping and display mode, as long as the bar is wide enough to show its name. Press **Escape** or click anywhere else to close the list.

## Highlighting an initiative and its segments

Select an initiative bar and the timeline highlights that initiative and every one of its lifecycle segments, wherever they sit, and fades everything else. Select a lifecycle segment instead and you get the same picture for its initiative: the initiative plus all of its segments, with the one you clicked still outlined as selected.

The highlight follows whatever you selected last. It ends when the selection does: press **Escape**, or click an empty part of the timeline.

Selecting an initiative that has no lifecycle segments, or a segment that isn't linked to an initiative, changes nothing on the timeline. There's no link to show.

### When part of it is collapsed

If a highlighted bar is inside a collapsed category, the category opens while the highlight lasts and its header shows **Opened for highlight**. The same applies to a collapsed group of initiatives hiding the one you're looking at. Both close again when the highlight ends. The highlight never changes which categories or groups you have collapsed.

### Where the highlight works

The highlight needs both ends on screen, so it works when the timeline is **grouped by asset** with [display mode](display-mode.md) set to **Both**, which is the default. Grouped by programme or strategy, or showing only initiatives or only deliverables, selecting a bar works as usual without highlighting. The deliverable count still works in every view.
