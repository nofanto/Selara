# Recording a Decision

The Decisions view is a portfolio decision log — a running record of *why* a call was made about an initiative, programme, or asset. It's separate from Version History (which snapshots the whole portfolio) and from Notes on an initiative — Decisions is specifically for capturing the reasoning and alternatives behind a choice, in a structured format.

## Opening the Decisions View

Click **Decisions** in the top navigation bar, next to Reports. The view has two panes: a list of existing decisions on the left, and the details of the selected decision on the right.

## Creating a Decision

1. Click **+ New Decision** above the list.
2. Enter a **Title** — this is required.
3. Optionally set a **Status**: Proposed, Accepted, Deprecated, or Superseded. New decisions default to Proposed.
4. Fill in as many of the remaining fields as are useful:
   - **Context** — what situation or problem made this decision necessary
   - **Considered Options** — the alternatives that were weighed, one per line
   - **Decision Outcome** — which option was chosen and why
   - **Consequences** — the tradeoffs or follow-up work the decision creates
5. Click **Save Decision**.

Only the title is required — you can save a bare decision and fill in the rest later by editing it.

## Editing or Deleting a Decision

Select a decision from the list, then click **Edit** to update any field, or **Delete** to remove it permanently. Deleting asks for confirmation first.

## Changing Status

A decision's status is changed the same way as any other field — open it for editing and change the **Status** dropdown. There's no enforced workflow between statuses; you can move a decision from Proposed straight to Superseded if that reflects reality. Use **Superseded** when a later decision replaces this one, and mention the newer decision in the Consequences field so the trail stays readable.
